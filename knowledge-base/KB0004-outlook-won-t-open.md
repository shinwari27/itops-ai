# KB0004: Outlook won't open

## Quick checks
1. Close Outlook completely, including from the system tray.
2. Open Outlook web at **outlook.office.com**. If mail works there, the problem is on your laptop.
3. Restart your laptop.

## Still not opening?
Submit a ticket. The approved fix resets Outlook's local cache; your mail stays safe on the server.
