# KB0014: I clicked a suspicious link

Act quickly. Reporting early protects you and everyone else.

## Do this now
1. Disconnect from Wi-Fi.
2. Don't delete the email.
3. Submit a ticket and choose *High* urgency. Security responds within 15 minutes.

You won't get in trouble for reporting. Waiting is what makes incidents worse.
