# KB0016: New starter IT checklist

Draft for HR and managers.

## Before day one
- HR submits the onboarding form at least 5 business days ahead.
- Manager approves access.

## Day one
- Laptop handed over by IT.
- First sign-in and MFA setup with a technician.
