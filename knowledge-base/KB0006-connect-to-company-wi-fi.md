# KB0006: Connect to company Wi-Fi

## Join the network
1. Select the Wi-Fi icon in the taskbar.
2. Choose **Contoso-Corp**. Your laptop signs in automatically with its certificate.
3. Guests use **Contoso-Guest** with the daily code from reception.

## Can't connect?
- Forget the network and join again.
- Make sure your laptop was restarted in the last week.
- If other people nearby also can't connect, report it as an outage.
