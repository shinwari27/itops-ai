# KB0005: Teams is slow, blank or not loading

## Try these first
1. Quit Teams from the system tray and reopen it.
2. Check **Settings → About** for updates.
3. Try Teams on the web at **teams.microsoft.com**.

## Clearing the cache
A corrupted cache is the usual cause. Submit a ticket and the automation clears it for you, then asks you to confirm Teams works.
