# KB0002: My account is locked

Your account locks after several wrong passwords in a row. This protects you from someone guessing your password.

## What to do
1. Wait 15 minutes. The lock clears on its own.
2. If you don't remember your password, reset it with **aka.ms/sspr**.
3. Sign in again.

## Still locked?
Submit a ticket. Our automation checks the lockout and guides you through a secure reset.
