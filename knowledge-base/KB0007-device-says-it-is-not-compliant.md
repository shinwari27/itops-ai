# KB0007: Device says it is not compliant

A non-compliant device can lose access to email and Teams until it is fixed.

## Common causes
- Windows updates are overdue.
- BitLocker encryption is off.
- The device hasn't checked in for days.

## Fix it
1. Open **Company Portal** and select your device.
2. Select **Check status** and follow the steps shown.
3. If it still shows non-compliant, submit a ticket. The automation applies the approved fix and re-checks.
