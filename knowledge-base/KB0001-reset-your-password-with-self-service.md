# KB0001: Reset your password with self-service

You can reset your Microsoft 365 password yourself, any time, without calling IT.

## Before you start
- You need the phone number or authenticator app you registered.
- If you never registered, contact the Help Desk.

## Steps
1. Go to **aka.ms/sspr** from any browser.
2. Enter your work email and the characters shown.
3. Verify with your phone or authenticator app.
4. Choose a new password at least 14 characters long.
5. Sign in again on your laptop and phone.

## If it doesn't work
Submit a ticket and choose *Password* as the topic. Never share your password with anyone, including IT.
