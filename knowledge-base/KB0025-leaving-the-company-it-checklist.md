# KB0025: Leaving the company: IT checklist

## For the employee leaving
1. Move work files you own to a shared Teams or SharePoint site.
2. Return your laptop, charger and badge to your manager or IT.
3. Do not wipe the laptop yourself.

## For the manager
1. HR submits the offboarding request with the last working day.
2. Approve it in the Approvals channel.
3. On the last day, IT disables the account, removes licenses and collects the device.
