# KB0020: Shared mailbox or calendar access

Access to shared mailboxes and calendars must be approved by the owner or your manager.

## How to request
1. Submit a ticket with the mailbox name and the access you need (read, send as, or full access).
2. Your manager approves the request.
3. IT grants access. It appears in Outlook within 60 minutes.

## Already approved but not showing?
Close and reopen Outlook. If it is still missing after 2 hours, reply to your ticket.
