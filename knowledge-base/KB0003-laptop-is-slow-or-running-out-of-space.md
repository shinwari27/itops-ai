# KB0003: Laptop is slow or running out of space

Low disk space is the most common cause of a slow laptop.

## Check your free space
1. Open **File Explorer** and select **This PC**.
2. Look at the bar under **Windows (C:)**. Red means less than 10% free.

## Free up space
- Empty the Recycle Bin.
- Move large files to OneDrive and set them to *online-only*.
- Restart your laptop at least once a week.

If the bar stays red, submit a ticket. Our cleanup automation clears safe temporary files and confirms the result.
