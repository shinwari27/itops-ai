# KB0019: Join or share in a Teams meeting

## No sound or microphone
1. In the meeting, click **More (...) → Settings → Device settings**.
2. Choose the correct speaker and microphone.
3. Check your headset is not muted.

## Cannot share screen
1. Click **Share** in the top bar.
2. Choose a single window rather than the full screen.
3. On a Mac, allow Teams in **System Settings → Privacy → Screen Recording**.

## Still not working?
Leave and rejoin the meeting, or join from the Teams web app at teams.microsoft.com.
