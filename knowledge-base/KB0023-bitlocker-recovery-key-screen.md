# KB0023: BitLocker recovery key screen

If your laptop asks for a **BitLocker recovery key** at startup, your disk is protected and the laptop detected a change (often a BIOS update or a new dock).

## What to do
1. Note the **Key ID** shown on the screen (the first 8 characters).
2. From another device, sign in to **aka.ms/aadrecoverykey** and find your laptop.
3. Type the 48-digit key.

## Cannot find it?
Call or ticket the Help Desk with the Key ID. Never share the key by email.
