# KB0008: Laptop isn't getting company policies

Your laptop checks in with Intune a few times a day to receive settings and apps.

## Sync manually
1. Open **Settings → Accounts → Access work or school**.
2. Select your work account, then **Info**.
3. Select **Sync**.

If it still hasn't synced after 30 minutes, submit a ticket.
