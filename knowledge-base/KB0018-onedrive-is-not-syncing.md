# KB0018: OneDrive is not syncing

## Quick checks
1. Click the **OneDrive cloud icon** in the taskbar. Is it paused? Click **Resume syncing**.
2. Check you are signed in with your work account.
3. Make sure the file name has no special characters such as \* : < > ? and is not open in another app.

## Still stuck?
1. Click the cloud icon → **Settings (gear) → Pause syncing → 2 hours**, then resume.
2. Restart your laptop.
3. Submit a ticket with a screenshot of any red X or error.
