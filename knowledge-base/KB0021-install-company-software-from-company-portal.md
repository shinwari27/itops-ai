# KB0021: Install company software from Company Portal

Approved apps can be installed by you, without IT, from **Company Portal**.

## Steps
1. Open **Company Portal** from the Start menu.
2. Click **Apps** and search for the app (7-Zip, Chrome, VLC, Adobe Reader, Teams).
3. Click **Install**. Keep the laptop on and connected.

## App not listed?
It is not in the approved catalog. Submit a software request ticket with the business reason.
