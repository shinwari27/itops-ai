# KB0024: Report a lost or stolen device

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone **immediately**, even outside working hours.

## Steps
1. Submit a ticket marked **Security** (or call the Help Desk).
2. Say what was lost, when, and where.
3. Change your password at **aka.ms/sspr** from another device.

## What IT does
IT locks the device, blocks your sessions, and can remotely wipe company data through Intune.
