# KB0013: Monitor, dock or keyboard not working

## Quick checks
- Try a different cable or port.
- Unplug the dock for 30 seconds, then reconnect.
- Restart the laptop with the dock connected.

If a device is physically damaged, submit a ticket and we'll arrange a swap.
